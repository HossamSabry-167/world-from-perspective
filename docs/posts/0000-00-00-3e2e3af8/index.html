At the End of the Line 30

2023-06-17

At the end of the line, I look back and I see:

Our teeth could've become harder,

Our eyes could've become sharper,

Our hands could've become craftier,

Our heads could've become clearer,

Our hearts could've become mightier,

We could've grown greater,

We could've become more,

We should've become more,

 

In God I trust, we can be more,

Avoid, as we must, the errs we made before.
